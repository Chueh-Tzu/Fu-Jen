學程定位The program

國際移動 × MBA 專業 × 數位創新 Mobility, management, and digital practice

自 103 學年度起全面英語授課。課程涵蓋國際企業、行銷、人資、全球供應鏈、財務與服務設計，並融入智慧營運與數位創新。最快兩年可完成跨國雙碩士。 Taught entirely in English since 2014, the curriculum covers international business, marketing, HR, global supply chain, finance, and service design, with digital innovation woven throughout. Dual master’s degrees can be completed in as little as two years.
