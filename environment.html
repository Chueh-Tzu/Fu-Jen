學習環境Our Learning Environment

在地學習、國際課堂：本地生與國際學生 1:1 合班，課程以英語進行。Study locally in a global classroom: a 1:1 local–international cohort, taught entirely in English.

國際化 MBA 學習環境An international classroom

學生組成除本地生外，尚有亞洲、美洲、歐洲、大洋洲等逾 30 國學生前來攻讀或交換。本地生與國際學生 1:1 合班上課，在菁英師資帶領下完成在地而國際的 MBA 體驗。

Alongside Taiwanese students, degree and exchange students from more than 30 countries across Asia, the Americas, Europe, and Oceania share a 1:1 classroom — an MBA experience that is local and international at once.

課程與學習焦點What you study

課程涵蓋國際企業管理、行銷、人力資源、全球供應鏈、財務管理、服務設計，並融入跨國企業所需技能與智慧營運、數位創新等產業趨勢。

Core management fields — international business, marketing, HR, global supply chain, finance, and service design — are paired with the skills of multinational operations, smart operations, and digital innovation.

走向世界的路徑Pathways beyond campus

可申請與 UM-Flint、KEDGE、IQS 的跨國雙碩士，或透過輔大全球姊妹校免學費交換，在兩年內擴展國際移動力。

Students may pursue a 1+1 dual master’s with UM-Flint, KEDGE, or IQS, or a tuition-free exchange through Fu Jen’s worldwide partners.
