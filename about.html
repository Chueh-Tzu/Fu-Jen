學程概況Program overview

輔仁大學是知名綜合型大學，歷史悠久，校友和姊妹校遍布全球。身為天主教大學，輔大擁有天主教大學聯盟、耶穌會大學聯盟、亞洲基督宗教大學聯盟等獨特國際網絡。

Fu Jen Catholic University is a comprehensive university with a long history and alumni and partner campuses worldwide. As a Catholic university, it belongs to distinctive networks including Catholic, Jesuit, and Asian Christian university associations.

2006 年與台大、政大同步，獲教育部遴選試辦商管專業學院，成立以實務化與國際化為目標的 MBA。為強化學生國際移動力，自 103 學年度起全面英語授課，並招收來自世界各地的學生。

Launched in 2006 alongside peer professional MBA pilots, imMBA has been fully English-taught since academic year 103 (2014) to strengthen international mobility and welcome students from around the world.

認證與排名Accreditation and ranking

輔大管理學院於 2005 年通過 AACSB 國際商學院認證，為兩岸首家通過的學院，並於 2010、2015、2020、2025 年持續通過。全球逾 16,000 所商管學院中，僅約 5% 通過 AACSB。

The College of Management earned AACSB accreditation in 2005 — the first on either side of the Taiwan Strait — and was reaccredited in 2010, 2015, 2020, and 2025. Only about 5% of business schools worldwide hold AACSB accreditation.

學程在 International Management 分類排名全台 Top 3、遠東地區 Top 20（Eduniversal 2025）。2021 年管理學院獲選教育部「大專校院學生雙語化學習計畫」重點培育學院，為大台北地區唯一獲補助的私立大學管理學院。

Eduniversal 2025 ranks the program Top 3 in Taiwan and Top 20 in the Far East in International Management. In 2021 the College was selected for Taiwan’s Ministry of Education bilingual education program — the only private university management school in Greater Taipei to receive the grant.
