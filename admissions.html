招生資訊Admissions

本地生每年兩次招生，皆免筆試。外國學生請依國際處管道申請。Domestic applicants have two written-exam-free rounds each year. International students apply through Fu Jen’s international admissions process.

國際經管針對本地學生每年設有 10 月甄試與 1 月碩士兩次招生（免筆試），適合不同大學主修、希望進入商管並強化國際移動力的申請者。考生須通過書審及口試；口試以英文為主、中文為輔。

Domestic applicants may enter through October screening or January master’s admissions — neither requires a written exam. Evaluation combines a document review and an interview conducted mainly in English.

兩次招生差異Two entry routes

  • 甄試：每年 10 月報名、11 月口試、12 月放榜；2 月（Spring）或 9 月（Fall）入學。已領有大學畢業證書者可提前於 2 月入學。
  • Screening: apply in October, interview in November, results in December; enter in February or September. Graduates may start in February.
  • 碩士招生：每年 1 月報名、3 月口試並放榜，9 月（Fall）入學。
  • Master’s round: apply in January, interview and results in March, enter in September.
