01

學程Program

Fu Jen imMBA is AACSB accredited. All courses are conducted in English. We offer two types of degree options:

輔大 imMBA 獲 AACSB 認證，課程全英語授課。學位路徑有兩種：

  1. Two-year FJU imMBA program;兩年制輔大 imMBA 學程；
  2. Dual Master's degree programs: M1 at FJCU and M2 go abroad跨國雙碩士：M1 在輔大、M2 出國
  • Choosing one institution from the following: the University of Michigan–Flint (UM-Flint, USA), Kedge Business School (Kedge, France), and IQS School of Management (IQS, Spain).
  • 可擇一：University of Michigan–Flint (UM-Flint, USA)、Kedge Business School (Kedge, France)、IQS School of Management (IQS, Spain)。
  • Students need to be admitted and pay extra full or partial tuition and other fees at partner schools.
  • 須獲夥伴校錄取，並另繳該校全額或部分學雜費及其他費用。
  • Students can obtain two master's degrees if the graduation requirements of two institutions are met.
  • 若兩校畢業條件皆符合，可取得兩個碩士學位。

學雜費與獎助Tuition

Tuition Fees and ScholarshipsTuition Fees and Scholarships

The standard tuition and miscellaneous fees for international students are NT$123,000 per semester. International students applying to the imMBA program may also apply for or qualify for the following scholarships, subject to the eligibility requirements and application procedures of each scholarship.

外國學生標準學雜費為每學期 NT$123,000。申請 imMBA 的外國學生也可依各獎助學金資格與程序申請或獲核下列獎助。

  1. MOE Taiwan Scholarship
  2. MOFA Taiwan Scholarship
  3. FJCU Scholarship for New International Students in Master's Degree Programs

For recipients of Scholarships 1–3, in addition to the benefits provided by the scholarship itself, imMBA provides an additional tuition reduction:

獲獎助學金 1–3 者，除該獎助本身給付外，imMBA 另提供學雜費減免：

International Rate → Local Rate

NT$123,000 → NT$68,660 per semester

For MOE and MOFA scholarship recipients, the local-student rate applies as long as the government scholarship remains active, for up to two years.

教育部、外交部獎助生在政府獎助有效期間適用本地生費率，最長兩年。

For FJCU Scholarship recipients, the imMBA local-student rate applies for up to four consecutive semesters. This tuition reduction is available only to students who qualify for and receive the FJCU Scholarship. A leave of absence or interruption of studies will result in loss of the imMBA tuition reduction.

輔大獎助生適用 imMBA 本地生費率最長連續四學期。僅限實際獲核 FJCU Scholarship 者。休學或中斷修業將失去此減免。

  1. FJCU President's Scholarship

Eligibility: New imMBA international students who meet either of the following requirements:

資格：新入學 imMBA 外國學生符合下列任一條件：

  • From an English-speaking country or a country where English is an official language
  • TOEFL iBT (72), IELTS (6), or TOEIC (785) score at CEFR B2 or above

Eligible students receive a tuition reduction of NT$40,600 per semester for up to four consecutive semesters.

符合資格者每學期減免 NT$40,600，最長連續四學期。

International Rate → President's Scholarship

NT$123,000 → NT$82,400 per semester

No separate application is required. The scholarship will be automatically applied and reflected on the tuition payment slip.

無需另提申請。獎助將自動反映於繳費單。

Students must enroll in the semester for which they are admitted and continue their studies without interruption. A leave of absence or interruption of studies will result in loss of the scholarship.

須於錄取學期入學並連續修業。休學或中斷修業將失去此獎助。

Important Note

Students may receive only one of the four scholarships.

四項獎助僅能擇一領取。

學分抵免Waiver

Course Waiver (postgraduate level)

Students who have completed professional courses in the imMBA program and wish to apply for a course waiver must submit their request prior to officially enrolling in the program. More details

曾於本碩士班修習專業課程、擬申請抵免者，須於正式入學前提出。 More details

There is only one chance to request and the deadline is in the first week of the class begins. A course waived or not is subject to the imMBA chair's approval.

僅有一次申請機會，截止為開學第一週。是否抵免由 the imMBA chair's 核定。
