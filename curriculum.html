課程與修業規定Curriculum and Requirements

AACSB 認證的全英碩士班。最低畢業學分 42（含論文 6 學分），一般需規劃 3–4 個學期。An AACSB-accredited, English-taught master’s. 42 credits to graduate, including 6 for the thesis, typically over 3–4 semesters.

上課時間表Timetable

每學期課表由所辦公告。請於開學前一個月瀏覽網站；選課細節於開學前三週以 Email 通知。The office publishes each term’s timetable. Check the site a month before term; course-selection details are emailed three weeks prior.

畢業修課規劃Degree map

請依入學年度適用之修業規定辦理。Fall 2025 入學起適用最新規定。Follow the degree rules for your entry year. Students entering Fall 2025 onward use the latest regulations.

論文指導Thesis advisor

每學期於 3 月底及 10 月初提供教師名單，由同學自行聯繫並確認指導老師。Advisor lists are released at the end of March and early October; students contact faculty directly.

入學前基礎課程Foundation courses

學程設經濟學、管理學（企業管理）、統計學、會計學為基礎課程。大學非商管主修的新生須於第一學期開學前主動提交學士班修讀證明（含成績與學分）。未於期限前提交者，應於第一學期至學士班補修其中一科；補修學分不列入畢業學分。擬補修課程須事先跟所辦確認課程名稱與學分。

Economics, Management, Statistics, and Accounting are foundation subjects. Non-business undergraduates must submit bachelor’s evidence before the first term. Otherwise, one undergraduate catch-up course is required in semester one (credits do not count toward the MBA). Confirm the course with the office first.

碩士班學分抵免MBA credit transfer

凡曾於本碩士班修習專業課程且符合資格者，得於入學當年度當學期向註冊組提出申請，並以一次為限。申請時間至開學後一週截止。

Eligible prior imMBA coursework may be transferred once, in the first enrolled semester, via the Registrar. Deadline: one week after classes begin.

修業規則摘要Degree requirements

  • 最低畢業學分為 42 學分（含論文 6 學分）。
  • Minimum 42 credits, including 6 thesis credits.
  • 基礎課程、專業必修、專業選修、論文撰寫、英文畢業門檻皆有規定。
  • Foundation work, required and elective courses, the thesis, and the English graduation threshold all apply.
  • 擬於當學期申請論文口試者，必須於當學期選課期間先加選「論文」。
  • To sit the thesis defense in a given term, students must register for Thesis during course selection.
