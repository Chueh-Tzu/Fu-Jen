◎國際經管imMBA招生管道

針對本地生每年設有甄試和碩士兩次招生，皆免筆試。

>>甄試招生：每年10月報名、11月口試、12月放榜，2月(Spring)或9月(Fall)入學。已領有大學畢業證書者，可提前於2月(Spring)入學。

>>碩士招生：每年1月報名、3月口試並放榜，9月(Fall)入學。

考生須通過書審及口試兩關審查。招生評委會依據考生提供之書審資料，如讀書計畫、英檢成績、歷年成績單及推薦信等綜合考量；口試則英文為主、中文為輔。更多招生資訊

◎考上國際經管imMBA需要馬上找論文指導老師嗎?

不用的。所辦於每學期加退選結束後皆會公告論文指導老師相關訊息，同學可依自身規劃選擇跟上的時間點。

同學通常需要三學期完成論文，一學期構思論文主題架構並獲指導老師確認，接下來兩學期與指導老師定期討論、實質撰寫論文並完成。

如果同學計畫出國攻讀雙碩士課程，必須於出國前完成論文。

◎碩一新鮮人該如何安排課程(含課程抵免)

基礎課程抵免：國際經管設「經濟學」、「管理學（企業管理）」、「統計學」、「會計學」為基礎課程，凡大學非商管主修的同學皆須於第一學期開學前，主動提交曾經修讀上述基礎課程之合格證明(含修課成績和學分)。未於第一學期開學前主動提交者，應於第一學期至大學部選擇其中一科為補修，以免影響第二學期碩士班選課。擬補修的基礎課程須事先跟所辦確認課程名稱和學分是否合適，補修通過之基礎課程不列入畢業學分。

碩士課程抵免：凡於國際經管修課且符合資格者，可於入學當年度當學期向註冊組提出申請，並以一次為限。申請時間至開學後一週截止，逾期不予受理。

MBA正規課程安排：同學應在開學前一個月瀏覽國際經管官網，了解修課資訊及畢業規定；所辦會在開學前三週以Email公告選課細節，同學必須依據內容跟上進度。更多課程及抵免相關資訊

◎Admissions channels

For local students, there are two admissions rounds each year—screening and master’s—both without a written exam.

>>Screening: apply in October, interview in November, results in December; enroll in February (Spring) or September (Fall). Those who already hold a bachelor’s diploma may enroll early in February (Spring).

>>Master’s round: apply in January; interviews and results in March; enroll in September (Fall).

Applicants must pass document review and an interview. The admissions committee considers materials such as the study plan, English test scores, transcripts, and recommendation letters; interviews are mainly in English, with Chinese as a supplement. More admissions information

◎Do I need a thesis advisor immediately after admission?

No. After add/drop each semester, the office posts thesis advisor information; students may follow the timeline that fits their plan.

The thesis usually takes three semesters: one to frame the topic and gain advisor confirmation, then two for regular discussion, writing, and completion.

Students planning a dual master’s degree abroad must finish the thesis before going abroad.

◎How first-year students should plan courses (including credit waiver)

Prerequisite course waiver: imMBA sets Economics, Business Management, Statistics, and Accounting as prerequisites. Non-business undergraduates must submit proof of having passed these courses (including grades and credits) before the first semester starts. Those who do not must make up one undergraduate course in the first semester so second-semester master’s registration is not affected. Confirm course title and credits with the office in advance; completed prerequisites do not count toward graduation credits.

Master’s course credit transfer: Eligible students who have taken imMBA courses may apply once to the Registrar in the semester of enrollment. The deadline is one week after term starts; late applications are not accepted.

Regular MBA course planning: Browse the imMBA website one month before term starts for course and graduation rules; the office emails registration details three weeks before classes, and students must follow that schedule. More curriculum and transfer information
